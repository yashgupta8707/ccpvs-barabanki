About the College

A centre of excellence for para-veterinary education

India's livestock sector supports millions of rural families, yet villages often lack trained professionals to look after animal health. City College of Para-Veterinary Sciences was set up in Barabanki to help close that gap.

Our programmes give students practical skills in animal health management, first aid for animals, vaccination, artificial insemination, livestock nutrition and disease control. Graduates can assist veterinary doctors, support farmers and take part in government animal-health programmes.

  • Practical, field-ready curriculum
  • Experienced veterinary faculty
  • Live livestock demonstration units
  • Separate hostels for boys & girls

Why Choose Us

Learning that goes beyond the classroom

Classes use case studies, field visits and simulations, so every lesson connects to real animal-care work.

01

Hands-on Laboratories

Dedicated labs for veterinary pharmacy, microbiology & pathology, pharmacology & toxicology, and animal nutrition.

02

AI & Breeding Training

Practice semen collection, processing, cryopreservation and artificial insemination under the guidance of specialists.

03

Livestock Units

Cattle sheds, poultry units and goat farming areas on campus for practical livestock-management training.

04

Clinical Exposure

Work alongside veterinary doctors at our clinic, which offers free healthcare and emergency first aid for animals.

05

Rural Field Training

Join animal health camps, vaccination drives, disease surveillance and rural extension programmes.

06

Library & E-Learning

Books, journals, online research databases and e-learning modules in veterinary and animal sciences.
