Campus & Facilities

A campus built for practical learning, with classrooms, laboratories, livestock units and a working veterinary clinic.

01 · Academic Block

Smart classrooms & lecture halls

The Academic Block has smart classrooms, lecture halls and laboratories that support high-quality teaching.

  • Four fully equipped lecture halls, each seating 100–125 students
  • Smart boards, audio-visual projectors and digital content for interactive learning
  • Wi-Fi-enabled classrooms that support online education and remote learning modules
  • Teaching through case studies, field visits and simulation-based learning
02 · Laboratories

Specialised veterinary laboratories

Well-equipped laboratories give students hands-on training in key areas of veterinary science.

Veterinary Pharmacy LaboratoryPharmaceutical instruments for drug formulation, compounding and analysis of veterinary medicines.
Microbiology & Pathology LaboratoryTraining in diagnostics, disease identification and microbiological analysis.
Pharmacology & Toxicology LaboratoryStudy of the effects of drugs and toxins on livestock.
Animal Nutrition LaboratoryAnalysis and preparation of balanced animal feed and supplements.
03 · Artificial Insemination & Livestock Training

Breeding, AI & livestock handling

AI LaboratoriesTraining in semen collection, processing and artificial insemination to improve cattle breeding, with hands-on practice under veterinary specialists.
Semen Storage FacilityA semen storage centre that preserves high-quality semen for breeding programmes and doubles as a training centre for cryopreservation.
Demonstration & Livestock Handling AreaA livestock unit with cattle sheds, poultry units and goat farming areas for practical training in disease prevention, vaccination and animal care.
04 · Veterinary Clinical Training Centre

An on-campus veterinary clinic

Our fully functional veterinary clinic gives students on-the-job training in treating animal health problems.

  • Free healthcare services for livestock and pets from the surrounding area
  • Students work alongside experienced veterinary doctors and para-veterinary professionals
  • First-aid and emergency care unit for injured animals
  • Real cases help students build confidence and practical judgement
05 · Library & E-Learning Centre

Resources for every learner

Our library holds books, journals and digital resources on veterinary sciences, pharmacology, livestock management and animal healthcare.

  • Textbooks and reference books for every subject
  • Veterinary journals and periodicals
  • Access to online research databases
  • E-learning modules and digital study material

Field Training & Internships

Learning in the real world

The college is working towards partnerships with government veterinary hospitals, dairy farms and private veterinary clinics to deliver a mandatory 3-month internship for every student.

  • Animal health camps and rural extension programmes
  • Disease surveillance and vaccination drives
  • Artificial insemination and genetic improvement of livestock
  • Veterinary drug dispensing and livestock disease management
