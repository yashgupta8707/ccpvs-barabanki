About the College

A centre of excellence for para-veterinary education

India's livestock sector supports millions of rural families, yet villages often lack trained professionals to look after animal health. City College of Para-Veterinary Sciences was set up in Barabanki to help close that gap.

Our programmes give students practical skills in animal health management, first aid for animals, vaccination, artificial insemination, livestock nutrition and disease control. Graduates can assist veterinary doctors, support farmers and take part in government animal-health programmes.

  • Practical, field-ready curriculum
  • Experienced veterinary faculty
  • Live livestock demonstration units
  • Separate hostels for boys & girls
