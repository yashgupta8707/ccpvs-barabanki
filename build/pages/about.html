Who We Are

Rooted in Barabanki, serving rural India

City College of Para-Veterinary Sciences is a para-veterinary diploma institute in Lakshbar Bajha, Pratapganj, in Barabanki district of Uttar Pradesh. It was founded to offer specialised education and hands-on training to students who want careers in veterinary support services, disease prevention and livestock welfare.

Livestock is a lifeline for farming families across Uttar Pradesh, yet many villages lack trained people to care for their animals. Our diploma programmes are designed to close this gap with practical knowledge of animal health management, first aid for animals, vaccination, artificial insemination, livestock nutrition and disease control.

The college is a unit of the Rajendra Prasad Education Society and is managed by Mr. Amrit Srivastava.

Our Approach

How we teach

Classroom learning is balanced with laboratory practice, live-animal training and field exposure.

Learn

Interactive lessons in smart classrooms using digital content, case studies and simulations.

Practise

Hands-on work in pharmacy, microbiology, pharmacology and nutrition laboratories.

Handle

Supervised training with live animals in cattle, goat and poultry units and at the campus clinic.

Serve

A 3-month internship with veterinary hospitals, dairy farms and clinics, plus rural health camps.
