Programme 01

Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy

The Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy (DVP) trains students to assist veterinarians with the preparation, storage and dispensing of veterinary medicines, and with animal first aid, vaccination and disease control. Training takes place in our pharmacy, microbiology, pharmacology and nutrition laboratories and at the on-campus veterinary clinic.

Duration2 Years (Full Time)
Eligibility10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Biology
Minimum Age17 years
Internship3 months (mandatory)

What you will study

Anatomy & Physiology of Domestic Animals Livestock Production & Management Animal Nutrition & Feed Technology Veterinary Microbiology & Parasitology Veterinary Pharmacy & Pharmaceutics Pharmacology & Toxicology Animal Reproduction & Artificial Insemination Preventive Medicine & Vaccination Veterinary First Aid & Clinical Practice Veterinary Public Health Drug Store Management

Skills you will gain

  • Drug formulation, compounding & dispensing
  • Animal first aid & emergency care
  • Vaccination & deworming procedures
  • Sample collection & basic diagnostics
  • Artificial insemination techniques
  • Record keeping & drug store management
Career opportunitiesVeterinary pharmacist · Veterinary assistant · Vaccinator · AI technician · Veterinary drug dispenser · Livestock health worker

Programme 02

Livestock Extension Programme

The Livestock Extension Programme prepares students to bring modern animal husbandry practices to farmers. Students learn breeding, nutrition, dairy, goat and poultry management, and how to run awareness and vaccination drives in villages. They gain field experience at health camps and with government animal-husbandry programmes.

Duration2 Years (Full Time)
Eligibility10+2 from a recognised board
Minimum Age17 years
Field TrainingVillage camps & internship

What you will study

Livestock & Poultry Production Dairy Science & Milk Hygiene Animal Breeding & Genetics Fodder & Pasture Management Animal Health & Hygiene Goat, Sheep & Pig Husbandry Extension Education & Communication Livestock Economics & Marketing Government Schemes for Livestock Farmers

Skills you will gain

  • Farm planning & herd management
  • Balanced feed & fodder preparation
  • Organising animal health camps
  • Disease surveillance & reporting
  • Farmer counselling & communication
  • Dairy & poultry unit supervision
Career opportunitiesLivestock extension worker · Dairy farm supervisor · Poultry farm manager · Field assistant in animal-husbandry schemes · Entrepreneur
