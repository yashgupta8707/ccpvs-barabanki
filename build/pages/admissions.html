FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What courses does the college offer?

We offer the Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy and the Livestock Extension Programme. Both are two-year, full-time programmes with extensive practical training.

Who is eligible for the Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy?

Candidates who have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology from a recognised board and are at least 17 years old can apply. Final eligibility follows the norms of the affiliating body.

Is hostel accommodation available?

Yes. The campus has separate hostels for boys and girls, each with capacity for 200 students, plus a canteen serving hygienic, nutritious meals.

Is there practical training with real animals?

Yes. Students train in our livestock demonstration unit (cattle, goats and poultry), the artificial insemination laboratory and the on-campus veterinary clinic, which treats livestock and pets.

Is an internship part of the course?

Yes. Every student completes a mandatory 3-month internship with veterinary hospitals, dairy farms or clinics, covering health camps, vaccination drives, AI and drug dispensing.

What is the fee structure?

For the current fee structure, scholarship information and payment schedule, please call +91 81770 01081 or visit the college office.

What can I do after completing the diploma?

Graduates can work as veterinary pharmacists, veterinary assistants, AI technicians, vaccinators or livestock extension workers, or in dairy and poultry farms. They can also start their own animal-care or veterinary drug business.
